about beth

practitioner,

the path

Elizabeth Faria has offered Insight Dialogue and silent meditation retreats in Brazil, China, Europe, and Australia since 2013. She has studied with Gregory Kramer and the Insight Dialogue Teaching Community since 2008.

She began practicing vipassana on long retreats in 1996, taking a two-year break from work to dedicate time to meditation practice in India, the US, Australia, and Thailand.

Elizabeth is a graduate of the Australian Association of Buddhist Counsellors and Psychotherapists Professional Training Course in Buddhism and Psychotherapy.

She has worked as a psychotherapist and psychologist for more than 30 years and has long been interested in the integration of the Buddhadharma with neuroscience and psychotherapy. Elizabeth lives in New South Wales, Australia.

Beth também ensina em português — para praticantes no Brasil e em Portugal.
